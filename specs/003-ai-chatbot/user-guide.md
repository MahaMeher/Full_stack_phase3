# Todo AI Chatbot - User Guide

## Overview
The Todo AI Chatbot allows you to manage your tasks using natural language. Simply click the floating chat icon in the bottom-right corner of any page to open the chat interface and start interacting with the AI assistant.

## Getting Started

1. **Open the Chat**: Click the floating chat icon (🤖) in the bottom-right corner of the page
2. **Type Your Request**: Enter your task management request in natural language
3. **Get Response**: The AI assistant will process your request and respond accordingly

## Supported Commands

### Adding Tasks
- "Add a task to buy groceries"
- "Create a task called 'finish report'"
- "Add task: call mom tonight"

### Listing Tasks
- "Show my pending tasks"
- "List all my tasks"
- "Show completed tasks"
- "What tasks do I have?"

### Updating Tasks
- "Change task 3 to 'buy milk and bread'"
- "Update the grocery task to include eggs"
- "Modify the first task to have a higher priority"

### Completing Tasks
- "Mark task 3 as complete"
- "Complete the meeting task"
- "Finish the shopping task"
- "Set task 'buy groceries' as done"

### Deleting Tasks
- "Delete task 2"
- "Remove the old task"
- "Delete the meeting task"

## Examples

### Multi-turn Conversation
1. User: "Add a task to schedule dentist appointment"
2. Assistant: "I've added the task 'schedule dentist appointment'."
3. User: "Change that to schedule dentist appointment next week"
4. Assistant: "I've updated the task to 'schedule dentist appointment next week'."

### Context Awareness
- "What did I ask before?" - The assistant will summarize your recent conversation
- "That one" - When referencing the most recently mentioned task

## Tips for Best Results

- Be clear and specific in your requests
- Use simple, direct language
- If you want to reference a specific task, include its ID or distinctive title
- The assistant remembers the conversation context within the same session

## Limitations

- The chatbot only works with your own tasks (not other users' tasks)
- Very long conversations may have limited context memory
- The assistant follows your requests but cannot make independent decisions about your tasks

## Troubleshooting

### If the AI doesn't understand your request:
- Try rephrasing using simpler language
- Be more specific about what you want to do
- Include task IDs when referencing specific tasks

### If tasks aren't appearing as expected:
- Check that you're logged in to the correct account
- Refresh the page to ensure task list is up-to-date

## Security Notice
All your task data is securely stored and only accessible to you. The AI assistant respects your privacy and only operates on tasks that belong to your account.
